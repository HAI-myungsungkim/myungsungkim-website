About

Research Vision

Recent work in neuroscience and psychology has produced surprising findings about emotion, intuition, and bias. I want to take that knowledge into healthcare, turn it into technology that people can actually use in their lives, and then rebuild what we know about the effects of that technology. Technology in healthcare has to serve two groups at the same time: the people who use it and the people who deliver it, most often clinicians. My doctoral research therefore splits into two questions — how users are changed by an intervention, and how providers come to accept that intervention — and I lean on qualitative and mixed methods because I want to catch the complicated context of real settings.

Recent Research

  • Preparing for my doctoral defense and finishing two studies with practitioner samples, both of which continue directly from the JMIR papers published this year.
  • A Delphi study with 20 counseling professionals on whether, and in what form, each digital intervention could be introduced into counseling practice.
